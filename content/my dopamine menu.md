## appetizers
- a quick meditation
- turn on music and dance
- get a cup of coffee
- do your skincare routine
- stretch
- make your bed
- get some sunshine
- quick journaling session
## entrees
- go for a walk
- do a workout routine
- bake something
- watch a movie
- read a book
- take a bath or an everything shower
- work on a craft
## sides
- listen to a podcast
- light a candle
- put a familiar show on in the background
- call a friend
- write an affirmation
- plan in your bullet journal
## desserts
- scroll social media
- play a game
- order takeout
- binge a tv show
- get a sweet treat
- do your nails

## specials
- book a vacation
- buy a new outfit
- start a new craft
- get a massage

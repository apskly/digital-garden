## how i got here
this is my small (currently private) adventure in digital gardening.  I wanted to try out the practice first without burning myself out trying to deploy a public system.

i learned about digital gardening from tiktok ([Great resources by talyssa](https://talyssa.com/) ) and explored articles that talyssa recommended.  through that, i found a great article by [Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history/). 

while learning in public is sort of paramount to the system of digital gardening, i found that trying to deploy the project slowed me down.  instead, i want to "garden" the [[deployment]] by cultivating my interest in web development and learning throughout the process.
